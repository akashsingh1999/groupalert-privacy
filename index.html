GroupAlert Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Who we are

GroupAlert is a group alerting app. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account info: name, email address, and sign-in details (email/password or Sign in with Apple).
  • Group data: group names, invite codes, membership, and alerts you send.
  • Device tokens: push notification tokens so we can deliver alerts when the app is closed.
  • Basic usage data needed to operate the service (for example, timestamps of alerts).

How we use information

  • Create and manage your account and groups.
  • Deliver alerts to group members.
  • Provide customer support and improve reliability.
  • Enforce free-plan limits and future Premium features.

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, and Cloud Messaging) to run the app. Alert content and your display name are visible to members of groups you join. We may disclose information if required by law.

Data retention

We keep your account and group data while your account is active. If you delete your account in the app, we remove your authentication account and associated user records. Groups you own are deleted; groups you only joined will remove your membership.

Your choices

  • Update your display name in Account.
  • Leave or delete groups you control.
  • Turn off notifications in iOS Settings.
  • Delete your account in the app under Account → Delete account.

Children

GroupAlert is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under 13.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the support email listed on the App Store listing for GroupAlert.
